# RZ Oasis — Art Director Review Set v1

Status: **approved by the owner on 2026-07-27**. Canonical copies now live in `../approved/`.

## Reference synthesis

The limited Oasis source set establishes a restrained monochrome/cosmic identity. The composition families use a neutral editorial grammar—type-led, big-number, hero-object, concept-photo, explainer, art-drop, data-card, lockup, and duotone—but every family has been rewritten for Oasis. No unrelated newsroom palette is carried over.

### Extracted palette

- Absolute black — `#000000`
- Ink navy — `#0D0E18`
- Midnight graphite — `#191A25`
- Structural graphite — `#222531`
- Orbit gray — `#4E4F5B`
- Lunar silver — `#A3A5A7`
- Soft white — `#F4F3F0`
- Verified-data emerald — `#20C98B`, reserved for source-backed positive data or a single continuity line

## Shared generation rules

- Portrait social poster, 4:5.
- Premium monochrome editorial design fused with restrained cinematic space imagery.
- Core motifs: eclipse, orbit, black pearl, lunar silver, monolith, gateway, phased systems, long horizons.
- Keep the bottom-left area quiet for the official Oasis logo to be added later.
- Never invent a logo, wordmark, contract address, QR code, wallet UI, market interface, token fact, price, percentage, or partnership.
- Planned gaming, metaverse, marketplace, or ecosystem utility must read as future-facing—not active or complete.
- Avoid generic crypto spectacle: neon cyan, rainbow gradients, gold-coin piles, trading dashboards, rockets, public figures, hype, profit language, and visual clutter.

## Concept prompts

### 01 — Black-Field Statement

Family: `type_led` / BLACK-FIELD STATEMENT  
Headline: **BUILDING THE NEXT LAYER**  
Prompt: Absolute-black editorial field, oversized left-aligned white geometric typography, one thin lunar-silver orbital arc, one small eclipse disc, broad negative space, calm institutional mood.

### 02 — Orbital Data Poster

Family: `big_number` / ORBITAL DATA POSTER  
Copy: **9.11%** / **ONE MONTH OF MEASURED MOVEMENT**  
Prompt: One large source-supplied number, curved silver eclipse boundary, rounded graphite module, one abstract emerald progress trace. No copied market UI, dates, axes, candles, prices, or additional numbers.

### 03 — Eclipse Hero Object

Family: `hero_object` / ECLIPSE HERO OBJECT  
Headline: **ONE UNIT FOR FUTURE WORLDS**  
Prompt: A single original, unbranded black-and-lunar-silver eclipse medallion floating in a dark orbital chamber, with brushed obsidian and pearl-silver materials and disciplined product lighting.

### 04 — Cosmic Civilization

Family: `concept_photo` / COSMIC CIVILIZATION CAMPAIGN  
Headline: **BUILT FOR A LONGER HORIZON**  
Prompt: A rain-polished black boulevard leading to a future city visibly under construction beneath a colossal eclipse. Architectural realism, sparse construction lights, no cyberpunk advertising, no claim that the world is live.

### 05 — Orbital Explainer

Family: `flat_explainer` / ORBITAL SYSTEM EXPLAINER  
Copy: **THE GALAXY GROWS IN PHASES** / **FOUNDATION** / **PROTOTYPE** / **WORLDS**  
Prompt: Central eclipse core, three clean concentric systems, architectural modules growing from sparse to connected, white/silver geometry, and one restrained emerald continuity line.

### 06 — Cosmic Art Drop

Family: `art_drop` / COSMIC ART DROP  
Text: none  
Prompt: A solitary obsidian monolith gateway on a cratered black lunar plain, framing a perfect eclipse. Museum-grade cinematic realism, vast negative space, no token, city, character, or vehicle.

### 07 — Roadmap Cards

Family: `stat_card` / DATA / ROADMAP CARD  
Copy: **A SYSTEM BUILT IN STAGES** / **FOUNDATION** / **INFRASTRUCTURE** / **FUTURE WORLDS**  
Prompt: Three staggered rounded obsidian cards with silver edges, abstract construction icons, and one emerald connector line. Conceptual roadmap only; no dates, percentages, or promises.

### 08 — Connected Worlds Lockup

Family: `lockup` / CONNECTED WORLDS LOCKUP  
Headline: **ONE ORBIT. MANY FUTURE WORLDS.**  
Prompt: A central eclipse core linked by precise orbital paths to six distinct abstract monolith gateways in one coherent black-and-silver system.

### 09 — Monochrome Resilience

Family: `duotone` / MONOCHROME ECLIPSE EDITORIAL  
Headline: **WHEN MARKETS COOL, BUILDING CONTINUES**  
Prompt: Anonymous hand calmly placing a lunar-silver foundation block at the base of an unfinished obsidian orbital structure, lit by a thin eclipse glow. High-contrast editorial photography, no market chart or profit implication.

### 10 — Long-Horizon Campaign

Family: second `concept_photo` campaign variation  
Headline: **A GALAXY IN THE MAKING**  
Prompt: A colossal black pearl sphere rising from a lunar horizon with an engineered silver orbit that is visibly incomplete, sparse stars, and architectural survey lines in the terrain.
