# TRIP Token - Candidate Approved Visual Reference Set

These ten images were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow from the user-supplied TRIP references. They intentionally contain no headlines, prices, charts, carousel counters, social-media UI, token coins, or generated logos. Production content must be added separately, and only the official TRIP footer lockup may be composited after generation.

## Shared production direction

- Premium travel-editorial realism with credible people, architecture, destinations, transport, and hospitality.
- Deep ocean blue, aqua, cyan, pearl white, restrained silver, and occasional natural sunrise warmth.
- Technology appears as subtle routes, ordered records, or integrated service architecture—not fake dashboards or generic blockchain decoration.
- Every portrait master preserves a large clean headline zone and a seamless bottom-left footer-safe area.
- Do not promise returns, instant payouts, refunds, insurance coverage, guaranteed security, verified ownership, prices, or delivered roadmap features.
- No invented TRIP emblem, wordmark, coin, domain, third-party logo, watermark, QR code, ticket text, or readable interface.
