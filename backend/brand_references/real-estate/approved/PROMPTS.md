# Real Estate Token — Approved Visual Reference Set

These images establish the brand's visual language. They are intentionally free of headlines, prices, charts, social-media UI, and unofficial logos. Production prompts must add current approved content separately and use only the official Real Estate Token logo/lockup assets.

## Shared direction

- Premium editorial realism with architectural credibility.
- Deep teal, champagne gold, pearl white, glass, stone, and restrained cyan light.
- Real property remains the hero; blockchain is expressed as elegant infrastructure, verification, or connectivity.
- Clean portrait compositions with usable negative space for production copy.
- No speculative price claims, currency symbols, fake interfaces, copied text, or invented branding.

## Samples

1. **Property Growth** — A refined property-development progression with modern homes, champagne-gold value columns, and a restrained teal rising path.
2. **Digital Real Estate Economy** — Diverse property professionals examining a physical-and-digital city model in a premium metropolitan setting.
3. **Verified Property Ownership** — A real modern residence with a transparent title-verification layer, emphasizing security and provenance.
4. **Physical–Digital Twin** — One contemporary home transitioning from warm physical architecture into an exact luminous digital twin.
5. **Global Fractional Ownership** — A realistic property district inside a connected global sphere, expressing distributed access without coins or money imagery.
6. **Accessible Property Participation** — An elegant everyday still life pairing a coffee cup with a precise modular home, communicating approachable entry.
7. **Verified Property Transaction** — A premium phone, embossed property deed, and verification shield connected by a restrained gold ledger path.
8. **Decentralized Property Records** — Hands protecting a luminous architectural model above layered transparent title-history records.
9. **Collective Property Participation** — A diverse group assembling transparent modules into a sustainable residential development.
10. **Sustainable Tokenized City** — A realistic coastal eco-district at sunrise with subtle teal network paths integrated into the streets.
