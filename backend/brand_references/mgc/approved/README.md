## Shared approved prompt contract

- Vertical 4:5 social campaign composition with a safe square crop.
- Signal yellow `#FFD400`, true black, white, and monochrome photography.
- Violet is permitted only as restrained gaming depth.
- Heavy geometric sans-serif with one short headline.
- The bottom-left corner remains a seamless empty region for the official logo applied after generation.
- Generic adult gamers and creators are permitted; public figures and third-party trademarks are not.
- No invented token performance, financial promises, price decoration, fake logos, or watermarks.
