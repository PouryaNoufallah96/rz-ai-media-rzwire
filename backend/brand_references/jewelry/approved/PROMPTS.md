## Shared art direction

Bright pearl and lavender fields; platinum jewelry; faceted lilac gemstones; frosted glass capsules; soft satin; high-key product rendering; creator-led lifestyle photography; deep ink-amethyst typography; spacious editorial compositions. Leave a clean lower-left zone for the official logo. Never invent logos, token interfaces, prices, charts, partners, listings, returns, live features, or production guarantees. Planned features must be visually and verbally framed as future-state concepts.

## Production constraints

- Preferred social crop: portrait `4:5`.
- Typography: bold geometric sans for headlines; clean humanist sans for support copy.
- Keep copy short and exact; add official branding after generation.
- Avoid dark cyberpunk, navy crypto clichés, rainbow neon, generic coin showers, excessive jewelry, fake app screens, and direct imitation of luxury brands.
- Factual fixed supply: `100,000,000`; do not invent prices, performance, allocations, APY, dates, or adoption metrics.
