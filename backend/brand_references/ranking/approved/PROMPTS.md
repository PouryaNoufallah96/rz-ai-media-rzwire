## Shared visual contract

- Portrait social campaign poster with one immediate idea.
- Deep aubergine `#20113D`, hot pink `#FF4F91`, mint `#55E6A3`, orange `#FF8A48`, and white.
- Heavy rounded condensed headlines, thick white keylines, rounded sticker pills, linked-node motifs, and crisp editorial photography or flat illustration.
- Use the supplied references as visual DNA without reproducing an exact layout.
- Leave the immediate bottom-left corner naturally clear for the official logo added later.
- No logos, wordmarks, copied game characters, public figures, crypto symbols, coins, wallets, financial charts, invented rankings, scores, dates, prizes, or watermarks.
