# RZWire workspace guide

RZWire is a private publishing workspace for five brands: MGC Coin, Ranking Platform, Oasis Coin, Jewelry Coin, and Industrial Token. Multimedia imports the configured RSS websites and Telegram news sources, analyzes editorial fit, routes cards to brand lanes, generates platform copy, and opens brand-specific image generation in Preview.

External Telegram and X posting, scheduled auto-posting, and Google Sheets writes are disabled in the local workspace. They must remain disabled until the new RZWire integrations are configured.
